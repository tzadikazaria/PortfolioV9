A street-food brand with an appetite for personality. Built to be seen, held and stepped into.

The aim was to create a complete, recognizable identity for a busy public environment. Bold typography, playful food icons and a warm palette carry the same character from takeaway packaging to signage and digital ordering.

Loud. Friendly.
A little hungry.

A playful Hebrew wordmark anchors the identity. Simple food icons and a compact color palette create a flexible language that can grow from a small sticker to a large environmental graphic.

Packaging / Patterns / Everyday brand moments

Beyond the bag.
Into the street.

Oversized graphics and strong color make the physical stand part of the identity. The system moves from handheld packaging to a space people can recognize and enter.

Same appetite.
A digital order.

The self-ordering concept brings the brand into the interface, using its visual language to keep the experience friendly and the choices clear.
