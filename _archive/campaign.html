Commercial campaign design for beauty and lifestyle brands. My work covered campaign concepting, visual design and execution, balancing expressive imagery with clear promotional hierarchy and consistent delivery across formats.

Seasonal campaigns, launches and promotional moments each need their own tone. A consistent hierarchy keeps the message clear while color, imagery and composition give each campaign its character.
