02 / Challenge

More than
a food stand.

The goal was to build a complete street-food identity that could feel bold and memorable in a busy public environment.

The system needed to work across packaging, signage, point-of-sale, menus and digital ordering while staying instantly recognizable.

03 / Brand idea

Loud.
Friendly.
Hungry.

The identity mixes bold typography, playful iconography and a warm street-food palette to create a brand that feels energetic without becoming chaotic.

04 / Identity system

Built to be
seen fast.

Large type, simple shapes and strong contrast help the identity remain recognizable from packaging scale to environmental graphics.

05 / Packaging

Packed with
the brand.

The packaging turns every order into a branded moment, using repeatable patterns, oversized typography and simple stickers to keep production flexible.

06 / Spatial design

The brand
gets big.

Signage, color and oversized graphics turn the physical stand into a recognizable landmark rather than simply another food counter.

07 / Ordering experience

From craving
to order.

The self-ordering experience carries the same visual language into digital, keeping the interface playful while making choices clear and fast.

08 / System thinking

One brand.
Every scale.

  • 01

    Identity

    A bold visual foundation that defines the tone of the entire experience.

  • 02

    Packaging

    Flexible branded surfaces that keep every takeaway moment recognizable.

  • 03

    Environment

    Large-scale graphics transform the physical point of sale into part of the brand.

  • 04

    Digital

    The same identity carries into the self-ordering interface without compromising clarity.

09 / Outcome

A brand you
can step into.

  • 01

    Recognizable

    A strong visual identity holds together every physical and digital touchpoint.

  • 02

    Flexible

    The system adapts easily from small packaging to large environmental graphics.

  • 03

    Experiential

    Branding becomes part of the complete food experience rather than decoration around it.
