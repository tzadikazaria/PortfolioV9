02 / Challenge

Clear fast.
Consistent
everywhere.

Each marketplace required product benefits to be understood quickly, often with limited space and different image requirements.

The goal was to create a visual structure that could adapt to different products and brands while still staying clear, informative and on-brand.

03 / System

A system built
to scale.

A repeatable framework made to keep listings structured across different products, categories and marketplace requirements.

  • 01

    Benefit hierarchy

    Turn key product claims into a simple and scannable order.

  • 02

    Repeatable modules

    Create reusable layouts that work across multiple product types.

  • 03

    Photography integration

    Combine benefits, product photography and textures into one system.

  • 04

    Platform adaptation

    Resize and restructure assets based on marketplace-specific formats.

Simple iconography helps shoppers understand key product benefits quickly while keeping the layout consistent across categories.
