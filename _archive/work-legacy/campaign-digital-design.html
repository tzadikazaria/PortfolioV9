Commercial work

Selected work

Multi-channel campaign design for beauty and lifestyle brands — translating one visual direction across social, web, e-commerce, email and paid media.

SocialE-commerceEmailPaid media

01 / Context

One brand.
Many moments.

The challenge was to create campaign assets that could move between different channels and formats while still feeling connected to the same visual system.

The work included campaign concepting, visual design and execution across e-commerce, social, paid media and email — balancing speed, consistency and commercial clarity.

02 / Campaign system

Built
to flex.

A flexible visual language made to stretch across seasonal campaigns, launches and promotional moments.

03 / Structure

The same idea.
Different format.

One promotional idea, shown as a campaign visual and as part of the social feed.

04 / Web & commerce

From campaign
to commerce.

The campaign language carried into the product page.

05 / Selected campaign

A campaign
in motion.

Rather than treating each deliverable as a separate design, the visual direction was adapted across formats so the campaign could stay recognizable from the first impression through to conversion-focused touchpoints.

06 / Channel delivery

Social → Web →
Email → Paid.

Each format was adjusted to its context while keeping the campaign’s hierarchy, imagery and overall visual tone connected.

07 / Working method

Fast. Consistent.
Scalable.

  • 01

    Campaign direction

    Define the visual mood, hierarchy and key message for the campaign.

  • 02

    Core assets

    Build the main campaign compositions before adapting them into channel-specific formats.

  • 03

    Adaptation

    Translate the system into social, web, email and paid-media deliverables.

  • 04

    Delivery

    Prepare assets in the formats required by each platform and campaign channel.

08 / Outcome

A visual system
that can travel.

  • 01

    Consistent identity

    One visual language across different campaign formats.

  • 02

    Flexible layouts

    Designs that adapt to different screen sizes and placements.

  • 03

    Commercial clarity

    Clear promotional hierarchy without losing the brand feel.
