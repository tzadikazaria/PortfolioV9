Selected work

Visual identity and mobile experience for guided tours inside Tel Aviv’s Central Bus Station — designed to make a complex urban space feel more accessible, curious and inviting.

Location

02 / Context

A complex place.
A clear way in.

The Central Bus Station is a layered, overwhelming space. The challenge was to create an experience that could help visitors feel oriented while also encouraging curiosity and exploration.

The solution combines a distinct visual identity with guided mobile flows, illustration and clear information hierarchy to make discovery feel approachable.

03 / Visual concept

Urban.
Bold. Human.

The identity mixes industrial cues from the station with playful color and character illustration, creating something that feels local without becoming heavy.
