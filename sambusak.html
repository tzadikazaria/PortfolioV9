Give Me
Sambusak.

A street-food brand with an appetite for personality. Built to be seen, held and stepped into.

The aim was to create a complete, recognizable identity for a busy public environment. Bold typography, playful food icons and a warm palette carry the same character from takeaway packaging to signage and digital ordering.

Self-initiated study — not a client project
Project
Academic concept
Focus
Street-food experience
Scope
Identity · Packaging · Space
Digital
Self-ordering flow

01 Loud. Friendly. A little hungry.

A playful Hebrew wordmark anchors the identity. Simple food icons and a compact colour palette create a flexible language that can grow from a small sticker to a large environmental graphic.

02 Beyond the bag, into the street

Oversized graphics and strong colour make the physical stand part of the identity. The system moves from handheld packaging to a space people can recognize and enter.

03 Same appetite, a digital order

The self-ordering concept brings the brand into the interface, using its visual language to keep the experience friendly and the choices clear.
